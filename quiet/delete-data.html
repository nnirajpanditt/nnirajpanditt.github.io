Delete Your Data & Account

100% Offline Device Architecture: Quiet does NOT use cloud databases, user logins, or remote servers. We hold zero personal data about you. All application settings, pinned favorites, and mindful delay timers reside solely within private sandboxed storage on your physical device.

How to Erase All Local Application Data

Because your data is never stored on our servers, you do not need to submit an email or wait for a deletion ticket. You have instantaneous, total control over erasing all data right from your Android device:

Option 1: Clear App Storage (Instant Reset)

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Tap on Apps (or Apps & Notifications).
  3. Find and select Quiet in your app list.
  4. Tap on Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and tap OK.

This permanently deletes all your locally saved launcher preferences, favorite app lists, and mindful delay timers.

Option 2: Uninstall the Application

Simply uninstalling Quiet from your Android phone automatically purges all private application files, cached data, and device preferences permanently.

How to Switch Back to Your Original Home Screen

If you wish to switch back to your manufacturer's launcher (One UI, Pixel Launcher, MIUI, etc.) at any time:

  1. Open Quiet Settings (tap the gear icon on the top right of your home screen).
  2. Tap "Switch Back to Old Launcher".
  3. Select your original home app from the Android system prompt.
