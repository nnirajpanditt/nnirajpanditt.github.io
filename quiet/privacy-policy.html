Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Executive Summary: Quiet - Dumb Phone Launcher ("Quiet", "the App") is engineered as a 100% offline, privacy-first Android home screen replacement. We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or analyze your personal information. Zero network permissions are requested. Your installed applications, phone usage, and settings never leave your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional launchers and social apps, Quiet does not participate in the surveillance economy. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics SDKs: We do not include third-party tracking libraries such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or Facebook SDK.
  • No Advertising Trackers: Quiet is completely ad-free. No ad SDKs or advertising identifiers (AAID) are ever queried or recorded.
  • No Cloud Sync: Quiet operates without servers. There are no remote databases or third-party servers communicating with your device.

2. Android Device Permissions & Policy Compliance

To perform its core duties as an Android device home launcher, Quiet requests only the minimum necessary local Android permissions:

A. QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES (Launcher Core Functionality)

Google Play requires a dedicated declaration for the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy:

  • Purpose: As an Android Home Screen (Launcher) application, Quiet must query installed applications on your device strictly to display, organize, search (via T9/Text search), and launch applications that you select.
  • Data Handling: This package list is read strictly in local device RAM during active use and cached locally in private device memory. It is never broadcast, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere outside your device.

B. Battery & System State

Quiet displays the local battery percentage and system time on your minimalist home screen via standard Android Broadcast Receivers. This state is computed instantaneously on your device.

3. Local Data Storage & Preferences

All customization data—including your pinned favorite applications, mindful delay duration settings, hidden applications, and theme preferences—are saved locally on your physical device using standard encrypted Android SharedPreferences.

This data remains on your phone and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the application or clear app data in your Android settings.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Quiet offers an optional, transparent one-time tip or lifetime Pro upgrade. All financial transactions are processed securely through the official Google Play Billing API. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card, debit card, or billing details. Google handles all transaction security under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Quiet does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because our application does not collect any data from any users whatsoever, it is completely safe for users of all ages.

6. User Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights regarding their data. Because Quiet maintains zero personal data on any server, your data is always in your complete possession on your own hardware.

To permanently delete all app data, you may simply tap Clear Data in Android Settings or uninstall the app (see our Data Deletion Guide).

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
