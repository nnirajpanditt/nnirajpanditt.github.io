About Quiet

Quiet - Dumb Phone Launcher is an ultra-minimalist text-only home screen engineered for Android users who want to break free from smartphone addiction and reclaim hours of daily focus.

Our Philosophy

Modern mobile operating systems are engineered by billion-dollar corporations to maximize your time on screen. Bright red notification badges, colorful icons, and algorithmic feeds trigger subconscious dopamine micro-habits, causing you to unlock your phone and doomscroll without intention.

Quiet changes the rules: by replacing icons with calm, high-contrast typography and pitch-black OLED backgrounds, your smartphone becomes what it was always meant to be—a quiet, dependable tool.

Key Innovations

  • Mindful Pause: A gentle 5-second breathing pause before opening distracting apps interrupts automatic muscle-memory tapping.
  • Pure OLED Black: True #000000 background turns off physical pixels on AMOLED screens, saving massive battery and relieving digital eye strain.
  • Instant T9 & Text Search: Swipe up to launch any installed app in milliseconds.
  • Zero Data Telemetry: 100% offline, private, and ad-free.
