Zero Remote Data Principle: Chakra Meditation & Healing operates without developer cloud servers or remote user accounts. All session history, streak counts, Japa mala logs, and sound presets are stored solely inside your physical device's local SQLite sandbox database. Consequently, you have complete autonomous control to wipe 100% of your data instantly at any time.

How to Delete Your Data

You can completely and permanently delete all your data using either of the two standard methods below:

Option 1: In-App Data Reset (Recommended)

  1. Open Chakra Meditation & Healing on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon) in the upper navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Privacy & Data section.
  4. Tap "Clear All User Data".
  5. Confirm your choice on the popup dialog.

Result: Your local SQLite meditation database, streaks, and preferences are immediately deleted and re-initialized to factory zero.

Option 2: Android System App Storage Clear

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps or App Management.
  3. Find and tap Chakra Meditation & Healing.
  4. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage usage).
  5. Tap "Clear Storage" / "Clear Data" and confirm.

Result: The Android operating system permanently destroys all local application databases and cache files.

Option 3: Uninstalling the Application

Because no data is synchronized to any external server, uninstalling the application from your phone automatically and irreversibly wipes all local application databases and saved preferences.

Types of Data Stored & Deleted

  • Mindfulness History: Meditation timestamps, completed session counts, and total minutes.
  • Mala Repetition Counters: Japa mala chant counters and bead presets.
  • User Settings: Custom session duration preferences, bell chime sound toggles, and notification reminder times.

Data Retention Policy

We do not retain backup copies or shadows of deleted data on any servers or external storage. Once cleared on your device, the data cannot be retrieved by our team.
