2. Information Stored Locally On-Device

All app state and user activity data are retained exclusively in your device's private sandbox storage using local SQLite databases and encrypted local preferences (SharedPreferences):

  • Practice History & Mindfulness Minutes: Completed session logs, meditation timestamps, and streak counters.
  • Favorites & Custom Presets: Saved chakra sound frequencies, customized duration timers, and interval bell presets.
  • Mala Bead Counter Records: Japa mala bead repetition counts (27, 54, 108, or free counts).
  • User Settings: Theme preferences (Dark Mode / AMOLED sanctuary mode), sound cue toggles, and daily reminder times.
  • Audio State: Current playback track position and volume level.

You retain 100% ownership and control over this data. None of these records are ever uploaded to cloud servers or remote databases.

3. Android Permissions & Purpose

The App requests only the minimum technical permissions required for optimal audio playback and mindful experience:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively to initialize Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. Core meditation tracks, frequencies, breathing guides, and mala counters operate 100% offline.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Ensures continuous, uninterrupted sound healing and meditation playback when your device screen turns off or enters sleep mode.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android 14+ (API Level 34+) to show media notification controls (play, pause, skip, elapsed timer) on your lock screen and notification shade during active sessions.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the delivery of local daily mindfulness reminders scheduled by you. The app does not send unsolicited promotional push alerts.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides gentle haptic feedback pulses when advancing digital Japa Mala beads.

Permissions We NEVER Request or Access

  • ❌ No Location access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)
  • ❌ No Contacts, Calendar, or Accounts access (READ_CONTACTS, READ_CALENDAR)
  • ❌ No Microphone or Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO)
  • ❌ No Camera access (CAMERA)
  • ❌ No Storage or Photo access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)
  • ❌ No Phone State or Hardware IDs (READ_PHONE_STATE)
  • ❌ No Biometrics or Health Sensor data (USE_BIOMETRIC, BODY_SENSORS)
