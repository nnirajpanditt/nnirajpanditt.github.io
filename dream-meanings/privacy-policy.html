1. Introduction

Welcome to Dream Meanings & Dictionary ("we," "our," or "us"), package name com.dreammeanings.dreamdictionary.journal. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and respecting the sacred personal nature of your subconscious reflections and dream recollections.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, our local-first offline storage architecture, and third-party advertising partners.

2. Local-First & Zero-Knowledge Dream Journal

Your Private Dreams Never Leave Your Phone: All personal dream logs, notes, lucidity ratings, timestamps, and emotional tags are stored locally on your device via an encrypted SQLite database.

  • We do not upload, read, transmit, or process your written dream narratives on external remote servers.
  • We do not sell or monetize your private journal entries.
  • You maintain 100% data sovereignty. Uninstalling the application or clearing storage will permanently erase your local database records unless backed up manually.

3. Information We Collect Automatically

While we do not collect personal identifiers directly, third-party libraries integrated into the app for advertising and analytics may collect non-sensitive diagnostic telemetry:

  • Device Information: Model, operating system version, screen resolution, and mobile carrier.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) used solely for frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • Diagnostic Data: Crash reports and ANR (Application Not Responding) metrics through Google Play Console to ensure app stability.

4. Advertising Partners & Google AdMob

To keep the application free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob uses pseudonymous identifiers and device cookies to serve relevant advertisements. You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID at any time via your Android settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

5. Psychological & Informational Disclaimer

The interpretations, archetypes, and cultural perspectives provided within this application are strictly for educational, cultural curiosity, self-reflection, and mindfulness purposes.

  • This application is not a substitute for clinical psychiatry, professional medical diagnosis, or psychological counseling.
  • Our content does not claim to predict future events with supernatural certainty.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is designed for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
