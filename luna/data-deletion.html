Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), this page provides instructions on how you can request or perform data deletion for Luna – Moon Phase Calendar.

🛡️ No Remote Account Data: Luna does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide an email address. We do not store your personal information, location coordinates, or lifestyle favorites on any external server.

1. How to Delete Locally Stored Data

All user preferences (such as notification times, saved lifestyle favorites, selected zodiac signs, and widget settings) are stored locally in your device's isolated application sandbox. You can permanently erase all local data at any time:

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Apps or Application Manager.
  3. Select Luna from the list of installed apps.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

This action permanently purges all local databases, saved preferences, and cached assets. Alternatively, uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored data.

2. Advertising & Analytics Identifiers

For users on the free tier, Google AdMob processes advertising identifiers in accordance with your consent preferences. To delete or reset your Google Advertising ID on your Android device:

  1. Go to your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
