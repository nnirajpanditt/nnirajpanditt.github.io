HairTrack is a privacy-first mobile tracking journal built for adults who want to maintain calm consistency in their daily hair-care habits, monitor shedding trends objectively, and capture private progress photos without anxiety or misleading claims.

📅 Daily Journal

60-second check-in for shedding observations, scalp comfort, stress, sleep, and hydration.

🌿 Gentle Routines

Customizable morning and evening non-medical self-care habits with consistency streak tracking.

📷 Progress Photos

Standardized 4-angle private photo journal with side-by-side comparison slider. Stored on-device only.

📖 Evidence-Informed Learn

Conservative, science-grounded education on follicle cycles, scalp hygiene, and when to consult a doctor.
