Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up daily reminder notifications?

You can adjust reminder schedules directly from the Settings screen in the app. Toggle reminders on and configure your desired times.

Where is my journal and voice data stored?

All affirmations, reflection logs, and audio recordings are saved strictly locally on your Android device's private sandbox storage to ensure absolute user privacy.

How do I backup or transfer my data?

Because the app operates 100% offline without remote cloud servers, data stays on your local device. Standard Android device-to-device cloud backup (Google Drive) handles app data when enabled on your phone.

How can I delete all data or submit a request?

You can instantly clear data via Android App Settings > Clear Storage, or refer to our Data Deletion Policy.
