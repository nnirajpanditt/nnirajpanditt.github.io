Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up daily reminders?

You can enable or disable daily reminders by navigating to the Settings page (the gear icon on the Home screen) and toggling the "Daily Reminders" option.

Where is my journal data saved?

All your journal entries and progress stats are securely saved locally on your device to ensure maximum privacy.

How can I cancel my subscription?

If you have a premium subscription, you must manage it directly through your Google Play Store or Apple App Store account settings under "Subscriptions."
