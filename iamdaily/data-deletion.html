Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

At Daily Gratitude: Affirmations, we prioritize your data privacy and give you full autonomy and control over all your personal data.

Offline-First Architecture: Daily Gratitude stores 100% of your personal data (journal entries, affirmations, voice notes, progress, and streaks) locally on your device's internal private storage. We do not store or retain your data on external developer servers.

1. How to Delete All Your Data Instantly

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you can completely and permanently delete all your data at any time without needing approval:

Method 1: Clear Application Data

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Select Apps (or Manage Apps / Application Manager).
  3. Locate and tap Daily Gratitude.
  4. Tap Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data). This immediately deletes all stored journals, recorded voice affirmations, and streak data.

Method 2: Uninstall the Application

Uninstalling the application from your device will permanently remove the application sandbox and immediately erase all stored files and preferences.

2. Requesting Data Deletion via Developer Support

If you wish to submit an explicit data deletion request or have questions regarding data privacy:

  • Email Address: nirajindia99@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Daily Gratitude - Data Deletion Request
  • Request SLA: We will process and confirm your request within 24 to 48 hours. Since we do not retain any user accounts or journal records on our servers, our team will verify that no external data is linked to your device identifier.

3. Retention and Automatic Deletion

Since data is stored purely locally on the client side, data is retained only for the duration that the app is installed. Once you perform a data clear or uninstallation, data recovery is impossible as no remote backups are retained by the developer.
