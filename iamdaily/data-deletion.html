Data Deletion Policy

At Daily Gratitude, we prioritize your data privacy and give you full control over your information.

1. Where Your Data is Stored

Currently, all your personal data (including journal entries, saved affirmations, progress, and streaks) is stored locally on your device. We do not store your personal data on external servers.

2. How to Delete Your Data

Because your data is stored locally, you can permanently delete all your data at any time by performing one of the following actions:

  • Clear App Data: Go to your device Settings > Apps > Daily Gratitude > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstall the App: Deleting the app from your device will permanently erase all associated local data, including your journal and streaks.

3. Cloud Sync / Accounts (Future Updates)

If we introduce cloud syncing and user accounts in a future update, you will be provided with an in-app "Delete Account" button located in the Settings menu that will instantly wipe all your data from our servers.
