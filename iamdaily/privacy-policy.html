Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026
Last Updated: September 2, 2026

Welcome to Daily Gratitude: Affirmations ("the App"), developed and maintained by Niraj Pandit ("we," "our," or "us"). We are strongly committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data practices. This Privacy Policy details how your information is handled, collected, stored, retained, and deleted when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Daily Gratitude is an offline-first application. We do not store, retain, or process your personal journal entries, affirmation records, audio voice notes, or streaks on developer servers. All personal user data remains entirely on your local device under your complete control.

1. Information We Collect and Process

We only access and process information that is necessary to deliver the features of the App:

  • Journal Entries, Affirmation Logs, and Streaks: All affirmations, daily reflections, custom manifestation notes, and streak counters you create are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage using private application sandboxes. We never transmit this data to external servers or third parties.
  • Microphone & Audio Recordings: The App provides an optional voice affirmation feature that requests microphone access to record you reciting affirmations. These audio recordings are processed and saved solely within your device's local application storage so you can listen back to them. They are NEVER uploaded, streamed, analyzed, or shared with external servers or third parties.
  • Technical & Diagnostics Data (Non-Personal): The App may utilize standard Google Play Services for app performance, crash reporting, and stability analytics. These services collect anonymous device metadata (such as device model, OS version, and crash stack traces) strictly to fix bugs and improve performance.

2. Data Retention Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, our data retention practices are as follows:

  • No Developer Server Retention: We do not operate external developer databases or cloud servers that store, retain, or maintain user personal data, accounts, journal entries, or audio recordings. Consequently, we retain zero personal user data on external servers.
  • Local Device Retention: All user-generated content (affirmations, journal logs, audio recordings, custom routines, and progress) is retained only on your local device for as long as the application remains installed on your device, or until you choose to delete it.
  • Automated Cleanup: If you uninstall the application or clear the application cache/data from your device settings, all local data is immediately and permanently destroyed.

3. Data Deletion Process & User Rights

We believe users should have absolute control over their data. Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have immediate and permanent control over deleting your data through any of the following methods:

Method A: In-App & Device Storage Clearing (Instant Deletion)

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps > Daily Gratitude (or Installed Apps).
  3. Tap on Storage & Cache.
  4. Select Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  5. This action instantly and permanently erases all journal entries, affirmations, voice recordings, and preferences from your device.

Method B: App Uninstallation

Uninstalling or deleting the App from your device automatically removes the application sandbox, permanently purging all associated local records and audio recordings.

Method C: Submitting a Data Deletion Request

If you have any questions regarding data deletion or wish to verify that no residual data exists, you can submit a request directly to our developer team:

  • Email: nirajindia99@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Daily Gratitude - Data Deletion Request
  • Response Time: We will review and respond to your request within 24 to 48 hours. (Note: Since no personal user data is stored on remote servers, we will confirm that no remote data exists for your device).

For more details, please visit our dedicated Data Deletion Policy Page.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for all audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since all data is stored offline on the device, no personal data from minors is harvested or shared.

6. Security of Your Data

We value your trust. Because your personal journal entries and audio recordings remain strictly on your local device, they are safeguarded by your device's built-in operating system security measures (such as screen lock, biometric authentication, and app sandboxing). We encourage you to keep your device operating system updated and secured.
