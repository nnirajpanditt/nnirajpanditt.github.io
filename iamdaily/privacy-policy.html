Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Welcome to I Am Daily ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide you with a personalized affirmation experience. This includes:

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous analytics data regarding how you use the App (e.g., sessions, streaks).
  • Local Data: Your preferences, saved affirmations, and journal entries are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • Track your daily streaks and progress (stored locally).
  • Improve the App's user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (such as Google Play Services or analytics providers) that may collect information used to identify you. These third-party services have their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

Because your personal journal entries and saved affirmations are stored locally on your device, they are protected by your device's security measures. We do not have access to this data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@iamdaily.app.
