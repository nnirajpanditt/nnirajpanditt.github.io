About Us

Welcome to Stoic Wisdom, an independent project born out of a passion for ancient philosophy and modern mental well-being.

Our Mission

In a world of constant distractions and anxiety, we built Stoic Wisdom to be a digital sanctuary. Our goal is to provide a clean, distraction-free environment where you can engage with the timeless wisdom of Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, and Epictetus.

Why Offline-First?

Your thoughts are your own. We designed this app to be strictly offline-first because we believe that journaling should be entirely private. There are no cloud servers, no account creations, and no data tracking. The inner citadel is yours alone.

Support the Project

If you find value in Stoic Wisdom, simply sharing it with a friend or leaving a review on the Play Store means the world to us. Thank you for embarking on this philosophical journey.
