Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Stoic Wisdom ("App"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The Stoic Wisdom app is built with a strictly offline-first architecture. We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data.

  • Journal Entries & Reflections: All your journal entries, reflections, and mood tracking data are stored locally on your device using a local database (Hive). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.
  • Analytics & Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, crashlytics, or background data collection tools that monitor your usage of the App.

2. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions on your device to function correctly:

  • Notifications: Required strictly for sending you local "Daily Morning Wisdom" reminders. These are triggered locally by your device and do not connect to external servers.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13, as we do not collect any personal data at all.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
