1. Introduction

Welcome to GlucoTrack ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal health data. This Privacy Policy details how we handle health and telemetry data when you use the GlucoTrack mobile application.

2. Data Collection & Zero Remote Storage

GlucoTrack is built upon an offline-first architecture. All health information you log into the application—including:

  • Blood glucose levels (mg/dL and mmol/L)
  • Meal entries, carbohydrates, macronutrients, and food logs
  • Target threshold preferences and reminder alarms
  • Historical health measurements and reports

is stored strictly locally on your device using encrypted local storage (Hive NoSQL). We do not operate external databases, cloud servers, or remote repositories for your health records. We cannot view, access, or sell your health data.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing maintenance, GlucoTrack integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive advertisements:

  • AdMob may collect standardized device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID and IP address) in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Zero health data sharing: GlucoTrack never transmits your glucose logs, food intake, weight, or personal medical parameters to Google AdMob or any third party.
  • You can review Google's advertising privacy terms at Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Ownership & Deletion

Because your health records exist exclusively on your personal device:

  • You have full data sovereignty to export your logs at any time as CSV spreadsheets or formatted PDF doctor reports.
  • You can permanently erase all logs at any moment by navigating to Settings → Privacy & Local Storage → Clear All Health Data inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in Android settings permanently deletes all local databases.

5. Children's Privacy

GlucoTrack does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The application is intended for individuals managing or tracking their wellness parameters.
