Data Deletion Policy & Request

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

How We Handle Your Data

The BMI Calculator app is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not store your personal health data on our servers.

All data you enter into the app (such as your age, gender, height, weight, and calculated BMI history) is stored locally on your device using a secure local database (Hive).

How to Delete Your Data

Since we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers, there is no remote account to delete. You have full control over your data directly within the app.

To completely delete all your data and history:

  1. Open the BMI Calculator app on your device.
  2. Tap on the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Look for the Health & Data section.
  4. Tap on Delete All Local Data and confirm your choice.

Alternatively, uninstalling the app from your device will also completely erase all stored data.
