1. Zero Cloud Server Storage

GlucoTrack operates strictly offline and on-device. We do not require accounts, logins, email verification, or remote server synchronization. Your blood glucose readings, meal records, and profile details are never transmitted to or stored on cloud servers.

2. Instant On-Device Data Deletion

Because all records reside exclusively on your local smartphone storage, you do not need to wait for a support ticket or manual server purging. You have immediate, one-tap control to erase all records:

  1. Open the GlucoTrack app on your device.
  2. Tap on the Settings icon in the bottom navigation menu.
  3. Scroll down to the Privacy & Local Storage section.
  4. Tap Clear All Health Data and confirm the prompt.

This action executes an immediate database purge across all local Hive NoSQL stores on your device, permanently erasing all readings.

3. Full Uninstallation

Alternatively, uninstalling the GlucoTrack app from your Android device immediately and irreversibly deletes the local sandbox storage and all associated databases.
