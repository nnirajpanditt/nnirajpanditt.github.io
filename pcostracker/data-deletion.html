User Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

Effective Date: September 9, 2026
Last Updated: September 9, 2026

At PCOS Tracker: Cycle & Health (developed by Niraj Pandit (Niraj Kumar)), we respect your fundamental right to control and permanently delete your health and personal data. This page provides clear, transparent instructions on how data is handled and how you can delete it at any time, in full compliance with Google Play's User Data Deletion requirements.

No Server Account Needed: PCOS Tracker: Cycle & Health does not require account creation, cloud logins, or remote server synchronization. All cycle logs, symptoms, lifestyle habits, and notes are saved strictly on your local device. Therefore, you do not need to submit an account deletion request or wait 30 days � deletion is immediate and 100% under your control.

1. What Data Can Be Deleted?

You have full authority to delete all data collected by the app, including:

  • Menstrual cycle logs and period history
  • Daily PCOS and wellness symptom records
  • Lifestyle tracking data (sleep duration, water intake, physical activity logs)
  • Cycle reminders and custom notification preferences
  • Locally generated PDF health reports and cached files

2. Step-by-Step Deletion Methods

Method A: Clear All Application Data via Android Settings (Instant Full Purge)

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on Apps or Manage Apps.
  3. Scroll down and select PCOS Tracker: Cycle & Health.
  4. Tap on Storage & cache (or Storage).
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

Result: This immediately and irrevocably erases all local databases, period logs, symptom history, settings, and cached reports from your device.

Method B: Uninstall the Application

  1. Locate the PCOS Tracker: Cycle & Health icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  2. Press and hold the icon, then select Uninstall (or drag it to Uninstall).
  3. Confirm uninstallation.

Result: Android will automatically and permanently purge the app's isolated private sandbox directory and all stored data.

Method C: In-App Entry Deletion

  1. Open PCOS Tracker: Cycle & Health.
  2. Navigate to the History or Log section.
  3. Select any specific cycle entry or daily symptom record you wish to remove and tap Delete.

Result: The selected entry is immediately removed from the database.

3. Data Retention & Server Purge Inquiries

Because the app does not transmit or back up personal health data to developer servers or remote clouds, we do not hold your personal records on any external server. There are no secondary server copies or backups to retain.
