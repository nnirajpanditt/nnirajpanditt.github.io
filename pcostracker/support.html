Frequently Asked Questions

How does the app protect my health data?

Your health data is stored strictly on your local device. We do not transmit or host your cycle records, symptoms, or personal logs on external cloud servers, ensuring 100% data privacy.

Can I export a cycle report for my doctor?

Yes! Navigate to the Reports tab in the application to generate a comprehensive PDF summary of your cycle lengths, symptoms, and lifestyle trends that you can print or share with your healthcare provider.

How do I backup or transfer my data?

Because the app is offline-first, your data lives securely in your phone's application sandbox. You can export PDF summaries for long-term records. Full local database export features can also be accessed in your app settings.

How do I request complete data deletion?

You can instantly delete all data by opening your device's Settings > Apps > PCOS Tracker: Cycle & Health > Storage & cache > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app. For more details, review our Data Deletion Policy.
