How to Delete Your Data

Method 1: In-App Permanent Data Wipe (Recommended)

  1. Open Angel Number Tracker on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the top right corner of the dashboard.
  3. Scroll to the Data Management section.
  4. Tap Permanent Data Wipe.
  5. Confirm the prompt by tapping Delete Everything.

Result: An atomic SQLite transaction instantly wipes all sightings, journal reflections, favorites, and metadata permanently from your device storage.

Method 2: Android System App Data Clear

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps > Angel Number Tracker.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

Result: The Android OS permanently destroys the SQLite database file and all local preferences.

Method 3: Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling the application from your device immediately deletes the sandboxed app database and all associated local files permanently.
