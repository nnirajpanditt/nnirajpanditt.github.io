Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data saved?

All data is saved locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. You can export your records anytime via Settings > Export User Data.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to the Celestial Premium screen within the app and tap "Restore Existing Purchases". Google Play will verify your active subscription automatically.
