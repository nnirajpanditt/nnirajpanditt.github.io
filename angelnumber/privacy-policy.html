Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0.0

All sightings, timestamps, notes, vibration ratings, favorites, and spiritual journal entries are stored strictly on your local device using SQLite. We operate zero cloud databases for personal records and zero telemetry tracking.

2. Information Collection & Storage (Local-First Architecture)

The App handles the following categories of data purely on your local Android device:

  • Angel Number Sightings: Sequence numbers observed (e.g. 111, 444), date/time stamps, context source (Clock, License Plate, Receipt, Dream), emotional vibe scores, and situational reflections.
  • Spiritual Journal Entries: User-written personal reflections, dates, and associated angel sequences.
  • Favorites: Starred angel number sequences and personal notes.
  • Local Cache: The 000–999 sacred codex interpretations pre-seeded inside local SQLite database.

Zero Cloud Retention: This user-owned data resides solely in local device storage managed by the Drift SQLite database. None of this personal information is collected, transmitted, or hosted on remote developer servers.

3. Network Requests & Live Codex Updates

The App includes a configurable HTTPS REST client used exclusively for checking if newer dictionary definitions are available for the public Angel Codex:

  • Requests to the codex endpoint send standard HTTP headers (such as If-None-Match / ETag) to verify library freshness.
  • No personal data is sent in these requests: Neither your sightings, journal notes, nor device identifiers are included in network calls.
  • Users have the ability to view, customize, or disable this endpoint under Settings.

4. In-App Purchases & Financial Data

Optional digital premium features (Celestial Plus) are processed strictly through Google Play In-App Billing:

  • We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, payment tokens, or banking credentials.
  • Payment transactions are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Premium entitlement validation is managed in a separate service decoupled from your personal SQLite database.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Analytics

Angel Number Meanings & Signs contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero commercial trackers, and zero behavioral analytics. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal records to third parties.

6. Data Sovereignty, Export & Deletion

You have full technical ownership of your data at all times:

  • JSON Data Export: You can export your entire sighting history, favorites, and journal entries to a readable JSON file with one tap via Settings.
  • Permanent Wipe: You can erase all user-created records instantly via Settings > "Permanent Data Wipe".

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not target or knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since all content remains on the local device and no accounts are created, no child personal information is collected on remote servers.
