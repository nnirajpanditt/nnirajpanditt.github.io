Data Deletion Instructions & Privacy Rights

At DayPlan: Habit & Daily Tracker (developed by Niraj AI Solutions / Niraj Kumar), we fully respect your right to data erasure and privacy.

Because DayPlan is an offline application, all personal data (habits, tasks, sleep logs, hydration history, and notes) is stored locally on your device rather than on our servers. Therefore, there is no remote cloud account to delete, and you have direct, instantaneous control over deleting all your data.

Option 1: In-App Reset

You can clear or reset specific logs or reset all application data directly within the app settings under Settings > Erase All Data.

Option 2: Clear Storage via Android Settings

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps (or Apps & Notifications).
  3. Select DayPlan (or DayPlan: Habit & Daily Tracker).
  4. Tap Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

Option 3: App Uninstallation

Uninstalling the application immediately and irreversibly deletes the local sandbox storage and all databases created by DayPlan from your device.

If you have any questions regarding data safety or deletion, contact us at nnirajpanditt@gmail.com.
