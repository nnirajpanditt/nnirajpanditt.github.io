About DayPlan: Habit & Daily Tracker

DayPlan: Habit & Daily Tracker (formerly HabitGrid) was created by Niraj AI Solutions (Developer: Niraj Kumar) with a singular mission: Privacy-First Productivity.

In today's digital landscape, many daily utility tools (checklists, habit trackers, timers, hydration reminders) demand account sign-ins, upload personal routines to third-party cloud servers, and expose user habits to continuous tracking. DayPlan changes that by operating 100% offline on your device.

Our Core Pillars:

  • All-In-One Life Dashboard: Instead of managing multiple standalone apps, DayPlan integrates 9 essential productivity and self-care modules (Habits, To-Do Tasks, Water Tracker, Sleep Scheduler, Pomodoro Timer, Plant Care, Breathing Exercises, Social Reminders, and Daily Overview) in one unified experience.
  • Total Offline Privacy: All your tasks, habits, and history are stored locally on your device's encrypted sandbox. No account creation, no cloud syncing, and no telemetry of your personal routines.
  • Fast & Reliable Alarms: Built directly on Android's native Exact Alarm scheduling to deliver timely, reliable notifications without cloud dependencies.
