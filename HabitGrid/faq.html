Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is my data stored privately?

Yes. DayPlan: Habit & Daily Tracker is 100% offline-first. All your habits, tasks, sleep logs, hydration history, and notes remain strictly on your Android device in an encrypted, secure local database sandbox.

2. Does DayPlan require an internet connection?

No. All core features (Habit Tracker, Daily Planner, To-Do Tasks, Pomodoro Focus Timer, Sleep Tracker, Water Intake, Breathing Exercises, Plant Care, and Relationship Log) work completely offline without an internet connection.

3. How do I backup or transfer my data?

You can export an encrypted JSON backup file anytime via Settings > Export Backup and import it on another device via Settings > Import Backup.
