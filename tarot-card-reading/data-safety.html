Google Play Data Safety Disclosures

Last Updated: September 2026

This page summarizes the exact Data Safety disclosures declared for Tarot Card Reading & Meaning on the Google Play Console.

Overview of Disclosures

  • Data Collected: No user data is collected or transmitted off the device.
  • Data Shared: No user data is shared with any third party or external server.
  • Security Practices: All local records are secured inside the device's sandboxed private app storage.
  • Data Deletion: Users have total autonomy to purge all stored data at any time via Android Settings.

Detailed Data Type Breakdown

Personal Info (Name, Email, Address, Phone, Race, Religion): Not Collected.
Financial Info: Not Collected.
Location: Not Collected.
Health & Fitness: Not Collected.
Messages & Communications: Not Collected.
Photos & Videos: Not Collected.
Audio Recordings: Not Collected.
App Activity & In-app Search History: Processed 100% locally on device only.

Advertising & Analytics Disclosure

If advertisements are activated, Google AdMob SDK operates under Google's standard developer policy and may collect pseudonymous device identifiers for ad serving and fraud prevention. No personal reading data or Tarot questions are ever provided to ad networks.
