Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to use Tarot Card?

No. Tarot Card is built 100% offline-first. All 78 cards, 50 spreads, astronomical moon phase algorithms, and ambient soundscapes are stored locally within the app installation.

Are my personal readings and questions private?

Absolutely. Your questions, notes, and drawn cards are stored strictly in an encrypted local database on your own device. We never upload or share your readings.

How does the Android Home Screen Widget work?

You can pin the Daily Card widget directly from the app or from your launcher's widget menu. It automatically reflects the latest Card of the Day, its orientation, keywords, and guidance.
