User Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

Last Updated: September 2026

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies on user data transparency and deletion, this document details how all user records, readings, notes, and preferences associated with Tarot Card Reading & Sacred Sanctuary can be completely and irreversibly deleted.

🔒 No Account Required: Our application does not require user accounts, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. Because no data is ever uploaded to external servers or cloud databases, you do not need to submit an account deletion request or wait for server-side processing.

Method 1: Deleting All App Data via Android Settings (Instant Wipe)

You can permanently delete 100% of your local SQLite database, saved spreads, stalker card records, and widget cache in seconds through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Tap on Apps (or Apps & Notifications / Application Manager).
  3. Locate and tap on Tarot Card Reading.
  4. Select Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

This action permanently and immediately purges all saved readings, journal notes, and configuration preferences from your phone storage.

Method 2: Deleting Individual Readings Inside the App

If you wish to delete specific readings while keeping your other history intact:

  1. Open the Tarot Card Reading app.
  2. Navigate to the Sacred Journal or Reading History tab.
  3. Select the reading you wish to remove.
  4. Tap the Delete / Trash (🗑️) icon and confirm deletion.

The selected record is instantly purged from your device's local database.

Method 3: Complete App Uninstallation

Uninstalling the application from your device immediately removes the app sandbox and destroys all local database files stored in app-specific storage.

What Happens to Stored Data?

  • Zero Residual Data: Since no data was ever sent to our servers, once local device data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, zero traces of your readings exist anywhere in the world.
  • No Recovery: Deletion is immediate, permanent, and cannot be undone.
