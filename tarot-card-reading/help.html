How to Begin a Sacred Reading

  1. Center Your Mind: Take 3 deep breaths. If desired, tap the Soundscapes player on the home screen and enable the 432Hz Miracle Tone.
  2. Formulate a Sincere Question: Frame open-ended questions like "What energy surrounds my career?" or "What guidance does my relationship need today?"
  3. Select a Sacred Spread: Choose from 50 spreads tailored for quick answers (1-card Oracle), timing (Past-Present-Future), decisions (Two Paths), or comprehensive life mapping (10-card Celtic Cross).
  4. Shuffle & Cut: Touch the deck and feel the gentle haptic vibration. Tap to draw each card into its assigned position.
  5. Synthesize & Reflect: Review the overarching guidance, life focus, and practical advice written in clear, warm language.
  6. Ask a Follow-Up: If you desire further guidance, tap "🔮 Ask Follow-Up" or "✨ Pull Clarifier".

Managing Your Journal

Every reading you perform is automatically stored in your local journal. You can add custom notes, tag readings by topic (Love, Finance, Career, Spiritual), and search your personal history anytime.
