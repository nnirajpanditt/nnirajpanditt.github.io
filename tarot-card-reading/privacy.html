Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Tarot Card Reading & Sacred Sanctuary (developed by Niraj Panditt), we deeply respect your spiritual journey and your personal privacy. We believe your sacred reflections, personal card spreads, journal entries, and life inquiries belong solely to you.

✨ Core Commitment: Our application is built with a strict 100% Offline-First Architecture. We do not operate external divination servers, we do not require account registration, and your Tarot readings never leave your physical device.

1. Zero Server Storage & Complete Offline Operation

The entire Tarot reading engine—including all 78 Rider-Waite-Smith cards, 50 sacred spreads, astrological moon phase computations, ambient 432Hz soundscapes, clarifier card logic, and personal reading synthesis—executes entirely on your device.

  • No personal information (name, date of birth, questions asked, or drawn cards) is ever sent to external cloud servers.
  • The app operates fully in Airplane Mode without requiring an internet connection.
  • We do not maintain remote user databases, tracking analytics, or profiling systems.

2. Local Storage on Device

Any data generated during your spiritual sessions is saved locally in a secure SQLite database stored within your device’s private app sandbox:

  • Reading History & Journal: Your past spreads, drawn cards, reflections, and tags are stored only on your phone.
  • Stalker Card Analytics: Frequency tracking of cards that repeatedly appear in your readings is computed solely on your local device.
  • Home Screen Widget Preferences: The Card of the Day, keywords, and Roman numerals displayed on your Android launcher widget are stored via standard Android SharedPreferences on your local hardware.

3. Device Permissions Used

We believe in minimal, transparent permissions. The app requires only:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used exclusively to deliver gentle haptic feedback when you shuffle the sacred deck or draw a card.
  • No Internet Permission: The app does not require internet connectivity to perform readings, compute astrological transits, or play ambient audio.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

If advertisements are displayed in free versions, they may be served through trusted partners such as Google AdMob. These third-party networks may collect anonymous, pseudonymous advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) in strict accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and local privacy laws. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children’s Privacy Protection

Tarot Card Reading is intended for general audiences interested in mindfulness and Tarot symbolism. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because the app does not collect personal identifiers, no child data is ever harvested or stored.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Indian DPDP Act)

Because all data resides solely on your physical device, you retain absolute, sovereign control over your information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can view all your saved readings directly within the app’s Journal and Sanctuary History screens.
  • Right of Erasure / Deletion: You can delete any individual reading with a single tap, or wipe all app data at any time via Android Application Settings. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
