About Tarot Sanctuary

Created with Soul & Purpose

Tarot Card Reading & Sacred Sanctuary was conceived as an antidote to noisy, ad-cluttered, and cloud-reliant mobile applications. In an era where apps constantly harvest private thoughts and demand subscriptions, we set out to build a sanctuary of pure tranquility and sovereignty.

Our Guiding Philosophy

  • 100% Privacy by Design: Your inner world, vulnerabilities, and spiritual questions are sacred. They should remain strictly on your personal device, with zero server uploads or external telemetry.
  • Authentic Occult Tradition: Complete fidelity to the 78 symbolic archetypes of the Rider-Waite-Smith system, illuminated by astrological correspondences, elemental energies (Fire, Water, Air, Earth), and cosmic lunar phases.
  • Warm, Practical Wisdom: Divination should empower rather than confuse. We interpret cards through warm, simple, actionable Indian English guidance—grounding ancient archetypes into real-life career, relationships, and peace of mind.
  • Sacred Audio Immersion: Every reading session is accompanied by offline harmonic frequencies, from the 432Hz Miracle Solfeggio tone to meditative Tibetan singing bowls.
