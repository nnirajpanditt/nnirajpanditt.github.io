Medical & Health Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 Application: ReikiFlow Standard: Google Play Health Content Compliance

IMPORTANT MEDICAL NOTICE: ReikiFlow is an educational self-care and meditation companion intended to support relaxation and personal contemplation. It does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, physiological curing, or healthcare advice, and is not a replacement for professional medical care.

1. Not Healthcare or Medical Advice

The content, hand position diagrams, timer routines, affirmation prompts, and Solfeggio audio frequencies provided within ReikiFlow are intended solely for personal wellness, mindfulness, and relaxation. No part of the application constitutes medical, psychiatric, psychological, or clinical healthcare advice.

2. Complementary Holistic Self-Care

Reiki is traditionally practiced as a gentle, complementary modality that works alongside conventional modern medicine, never in place of it. ReikiFlow does not claim to cure, heal, diagnose, treat, or prevent any illness, disease, chronic pain, or mental health disorder.

3. Consult Licensed Healthcare Professionals

You should always consult a licensed physician, psychotherapist, or qualified healthcare professional before beginning any new wellness routine, particularly if you have pre-existing medical conditions, chronic pain, psychological disorders, or acute symptoms.

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking clinical treatment because of information, audio, or timer exercises experienced within ReikiFlow.

4. Sound & Frequency Caution

ReikiFlow features meditative soundscapes, Tibetan singing bowls, and Solfeggio tones (including 432Hz and 528Hz). If you experience dizziness, disorientation, headaches, tinnitus, or auditory discomfort while using audio features, cease playback immediately and adjust volume levels. Do not use audio headphones at excessive volumes.

5. Emergency Medical Situations

In Case of an Emergency: If you are experiencing a medical emergency, acute physical trauma, severe psychological distress, or suicidal ideation, please dial your local emergency number (such as 911, 112, or local crisis hotline) or seek immediate medical attention from an emergency hospital facility.

6. Google Play Policy Statement

This disclaimer is published in accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies on Health Content and Services, ensuring complete transparency, ethical communication, and the elimination of false or unverified health claims.
