About ReikiFlow & Developer

Developer: Niraj Pandit Project: ReikiFlow Holistic Companion Focus: Offline-First Mindfulness Technology

Our Vision

ReikiFlow was created with a single uncompromising mission: to provide practitioners, masters, and seekers with a sacred, distraction-free companion for daily Usui Reiki practice and meditation.

Most contemporary mindfulness applications are cluttered with social feeds, invasive subscriptions, aggressive advertising, and cloud tracking. We believe that spiritual contemplation and energetic healing demand a sanctuary of calm—grounded in simplicity, privacy, and reverence.

The Five Reiki Principles (Gokai)

The philosophy of ReikiFlow is deeply rooted in the Five Precepts passed down by Mikao Usui Sensei:

Just for today (Kyō dake wa):

1. Do not be angry (Ikaru-na)

2. Do not worry (Shinpai suna)

3. Be grateful (Kansha shite)

4. Work with diligence (Gyō o hageme)

5. Be kind to people (Hito ni shinsetsu ni)

Design Ethics & Engineering Principles

  • Offline-First Sovereignty: Your personal reflections, practice minutes, and habits belong exclusively to you. No cloud databases, no telemetry, no tracking.
  • True Eyes-Closed Usui Utility: Real Reiki sessions require placing hands on your body or aura with eyes closed. We engineered multi-stage acoustic chimes (Tibetan bowls, Japanese Rin gong, Tingsha) and tactile dual-vibrations so you never need to break concentration to glance at a screen.
  • Harmonic Audio Synthesis: Pure mathematically aligned Solfeggio tones (396Hz through 963Hz) and calming 432Hz/528Hz drones built directly into the sound engine.
