Data Deletion Policy & Request

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 Application: ReikiFlow (Package: com.reikiflow.reikitimer.meditation) Google Play Requirement: Data Safety Compliance

Key Guarantee: ReikiFlow operates on an offline-first architecture. We do not store your data on external cloud servers, meaning you have instantaneous, permanent control to delete 100% of your records directly on your device at any moment without delay.

1. Types of Data Stored by ReikiFlow

All data created during your practice is stored exclusively on your device in local SQLite tables and Android shared preferences:

  • Practice History: Timestamps, duration, and completed hand positions.
  • Meditation Logs: Meditative session records.
  • Journal Entries: Personal reflection notes recorded after sessions.
  • Favorites & Custom Routines: Custom timers created in the Routine Builder.
  • Milestone Achievements: On-device streak tracking and achievement badges.
  • User Preferences: Theme selection, interval bell sound, volume, and reminder time.

2. How to Delete Your Data Instantly (In-App)

You do not need to wait for developer processing to erase your data. You can delete all your records permanently within the application:

  1. Open the ReikiFlow application on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Data Management section.
  4. Choose your desired deletion scope:
    • Clear Practice History: Deletes all session and meditation logs while preserving your favorite custom routines and settings.
    • Reset All Progress & App Data: Permanently erases all tables, deletes custom routines, resets achievements, and returns the app to a fresh installation state.
  5. Confirm your choice in the confirmation dialog. Deletion occurs immediately.

3. Alternative Device-Level Deletion

You can also delete all app data using standard Android operating system controls:

  • Option A: Clear App Storage: Open Android Settings → Apps → ReikiFlow → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Option B: Uninstall the App: Uninstalling ReikiFlow from your Android device permanently destroys the app's sandboxed SQLite database file (reikiflow.db) and all preference files.

4. Requesting Data Verification via Developer

Because we maintain no remote server infrastructure, user accounts, or central databases, we hold no off-device copies of your information. There is no remote account to delete.

If you have any questions, need assistance with data removal, or wish to verify data handling compliance, you may email the developer directly:

This public page fulfills Google Play's User Data & Data Deletion Policy, ensuring that all users are fully informed of how their data is stored, how it can be deleted, and their complete autonomy over their personal records.
