Data Deletion Instructions

CSE Plus respects your privacy and gives you full control over your data. All of your learning progress, bookmarks, and statistics are stored locally on your mobile device.

How to delete your data:

Since your data is not stored on any external servers, you can instantly and permanently delete all your user data by following these steps:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps or App Management.
  3. Find and select CSE Plus from the list.
  4. Tap on Storage and Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Data or Clear Storage.

Alternatively, simply uninstalling the application will automatically remove all associated local data from your device.
