Help Center & User Guides

Need assistance navigating the app? Review our detailed feature guides below to get the most out of your CSE Plus experience!

Navigating the Learning Dashboard

The Learning Dashboard is your central hub. It automatically tracks your daily engagement. To keep your "Study Streak" alive, you must open the app and complete at least one activity (reading a note, solving a program, or taking an MCQ) every 24 hours. The dashboard also displays your unlocked achievements, which are awarded for hitting milestones like solving 50 MCQs or completing a mock test.

Mastering the MCQ Mock Tests

To simulate a real exam environment:

  1. Navigate to the MCQ tab at the bottom of your screen.
  2. Tap on the floating action button to enter Exam Mode.
  3. Select your difficulty and the number of questions.
  4. A countdown timer will begin. You must submit your answers before the time runs out! Any questions you get wrong will be automatically saved to your "Wrong Answers" vault for later review.

Using the Revision Center (Bookmarks)

If you encounter a particularly difficult Program or Viva question, tap the ⭐ (Star) icon in the top right corner of the screen. This immediately saves the item to your unified Bookmarks list. You can access all your bookmarked content from the Dashboard, making it incredibly easy to cram right before an exam!

Enabling Dark Mode

Late-night coding session? Protect your eyes by enabling Dark Mode. Go to the Settings tab, tap on Theme, and toggle Dark Mode on. The entire app will instantly switch to a beautifully curated dark color palette designed to reduce eye strain.
