Help Center

Need assistance navigating the app? Here are some quick guides:

  • Mock Tests: To start a mock test, navigate to the Exam Mode tab and select a difficulty. Ensure you finish before the timer runs out!
  • Dark Mode: You can toggle Dark Mode on or off by visiting the Settings tab and selecting 'Theme'.
  • Bookmarks: Found a difficult question? Tap the star icon on any program or MCQ to save it to your Revision Center.
