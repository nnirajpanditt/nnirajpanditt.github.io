About CSE Plus

Our Mission

Welcome to CSE Plus! Our overarching mission is to democratize high-quality computer science education by making it universally accessible, engaging, and resilient to internet connectivity issues. We believe that every student, regardless of their geographical location or internet bandwidth, deserves access to top-tier technical resources, interview preparation tools, and comprehensive coding guides.

Why We Built CSE Plus

During university, many computer science students struggle with scattered resources. Notes are on one website, practice programs are on another, and mock tests require expensive subscriptions. CSE Plus was born out of a desire to consolidate the entire CSE curriculum into a single, lightning-fast mobile application. We focused heavily on the Offline-First experience because we know that students often do their best studying during commutes or in campus libraries with spotty Wi-Fi.

Meet the Developer

CSE Plus is passionately developed and maintained by Niraj Pandit, an experienced Android and Flutter software engineer. Niraj recognized the friction points in traditional tech education and utilized the power of the Flutter framework and local SQLite databases to architect an app that is not only beautiful and performant but deeply respectful of user privacy and data usage.

By keeping the app completely free and ad-supported, Niraj hopes to impact thousands of students globally, helping them secure their dream jobs in the tech industry.
