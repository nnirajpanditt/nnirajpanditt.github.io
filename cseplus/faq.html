Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No! CSE Plus is built with an "Offline-First" architecture. Once you download the app, all study materials, notes, and MCQs are available completely offline.

Is CSE Plus free?

Yes, the app is completely free to download and use.

How is my data stored?

All your progress, bookmarks, and streaks are securely saved directly on your device using a local SQLite database. We do not collect or upload your personal study data to any cloud servers.

How do I reset my progress?

You can reset all your progress by going to the Settings page in the app and tapping "Reset Progress & Clear Data".
